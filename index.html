Drum Machine – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Drum Machine ("the App") is an Android application that provides drum patterns and musical accompaniment for personal practice and live music sessions.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use the App and how third-party advertising services may process information.

1. Information handled by the App

Drum Machine does not require you to create an account. The developer does not operate its own server or remote database to store your personal information.

The App may store information locally on your device, including:

  • App preferences and settings.
  • Selected drum kits, patterns, tempo and playback preferences.
  • Custom drum kits and audio samples that you choose to import.

Imported audio samples and custom kits are stored locally. They are not uploaded to the developer's servers.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements in Practice mode. Live mode is designed not to request or display advertisements.

When advertising services are used, Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers and device identifiers.
  • IP address and approximate location information derived from it.
  • App interactions and advertising activity.
  • Diagnostic, fraud-prevention and ad-performance information.

This information may be used to provide advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud and comply with legal requirements.

Depending on your location, applicable laws and consent choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

For more information about Google's data practices, visit: Google Privacy Policy.

3. Consent and privacy choices

Where required, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent.

You can review or change your advertising privacy choices through the "Ad privacy options" section in the App's settings, when that option is available.

You can also manage advertising preferences through your Google account and Android device settings.

4. Data sharing

The developer does not sell your personal information.

Information processed by Google Mobile Ads SDK may be shared with Google and its advertising partners as necessary to provide advertising services, measure performance, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.

These third parties process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

5. Data storage and deletion

App preferences and imported custom kits are stored locally on your device.

You can delete locally stored App data through Android settings or by uninstalling the App. Device backups and operating-system behavior may affect whether some information is retained.

Information processed by Google through its advertising services is subject to Google's own retention and deletion practices.

6. Children's privacy

Drum Machine is not specifically designed for children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

7. Security

The App is designed to operate primarily on your device and does not maintain a developer-operated account system or remote database.

Third-party services, including Google AdMob, operate under their own security and privacy practices.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App's functionality, advertising services or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published at this same URL with a revised effective date.
