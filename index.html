Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Drum Machine ("the App"), developed by Drum Machine ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

Drum Machine does not require users to create an account. We do not operate our own server or remote database to store users' personal information.

The App stores certain information locally on your device, such as app preferences and custom drum kits imported by you. This information remains on your device and is not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

The App does not directly request access to your precise physical location, contacts, calendar, or personal messages.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. AdMob is provided by Google and may collect and process certain information to provide, measure, and improve advertising services.

Depending on your device, settings, region, and consent choices, information processed by Google may include:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • App interactions and advertising activity.
  • Diagnostics, crash information, and performance data.

Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and other purposes described in its own privacy policies.

We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), where applicable, to request and manage advertising consent and privacy choices.

For more information about Google's data practices, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

3. Custom Drum Kits and Imported Files

Drum Machine allows users to import their own WAV audio files to create custom drum kits.

Imported files and custom kit information are stored locally on your device. The App does not upload these files to our servers or share them with other users.

4. Data Storage and Security

Preferences and custom content managed by the App are stored locally on your device.

We do not operate a remote account system or database. Information processed by third-party services, such as Google AdMob, is handled according to their respective privacy and security practices.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored preferences and imported custom kits remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App, subject to your device's Android storage behavior and backups.

Information collected by Google AdMob is subject to Google's retention and deletion policies.

6. Children's Privacy

Drum Machine is not specifically designed for or directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising services may apply their own protections and requirements according to applicable laws and user settings.

7. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your region, you may be presented with options to manage or withdraw consent for advertising-related data processing.

You can also manage your device's advertising settings through Android settings, where available.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page, together with the revised update date.
