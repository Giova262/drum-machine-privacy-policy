2. Summary

  • The App has no user accounts. We do not operate our own server, and we do not sync or store your personal information on any server of ours.
  • Your settings and the drum kits you create stay on your device.
  • The App shows banner advertisements from Google AdMob in both Practice mode and Live mode. Google collects certain information from your device to show those ads, as explained in section 4.
  • Where the rules of your region require it, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent message before requesting ads, and lets you change your choices later.

3. Information that stays on your device

The App itself does not send us any information. It keeps the following on your device:

  • Settings: theme, language, volume, and the options of the automatic sections (whether they are on and how many bars each section lasts).
  • Custom drum kits: if you import your own WAV audio files, you choose them through the Android file picker. The App only reads the files you select and keeps a copy of them, with the names and assignments of your kits, in its private storage on your device. They are never uploaded or shared with other users.
  • Advertising consent choices: the choices you make in Google's consent message are stored on your device by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), so that they are respected the next time you open the App.

Device backups: if you have turned on Android's backup on your device, the information the App keeps on your device, except custom drum kits, may be included in that backup, which is handled by your device and your Google account according to your own settings. Custom drum kits are excluded from backups and from transfers to a new device.

Permissions: the App can show a notification with controls to stop the drums while it plays in the background. On recent Android versions it asks for your permission to show notifications, and it works without it. Google's advertising software included in the App also declares permissions to access the internet and the network state, the advertising ID, and Android's advertising services. The App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, calendar, or messages.

We do not use our own analytics or crash-reporting services. Google Play may provide us with aggregated statistics about the App and technical reports about its crashes or freezes; we do not use them to identify anyone.

4. Advertising: Google AdMob

The App shows banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google, in both Practice mode and Live mode. Ads in the App are muted. The App requests ads only after Google's consent tools allow it in your region, as described in section 5.

According to Google's description of the version of its Google Mobile Ads SDK included in the App (25.5.0), the SDK automatically collects and shares the following information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention purposes:

Category What it includes, as described by Google Purposes
IP address and approximate location Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of the device. Advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, including estimating the general location of the device.
App interactions Interactions with the App, such as app launches, taps, and video views. Advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.
Diagnostic information Information about the performance of the App and of Google's software, such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage. Advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.
Device and advertising identifiers The Android advertising ID, the app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device. Advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Google AdMob collects this information, including your interactions with the App, directly from your device. We do not receive individual information that identifies you: we only have access to aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown and the resulting earnings. Google states that the information its SDK collects is encrypted in transit.

Recipients: this information is collected by Google, which processes it as described in its own privacy policy. Where Google's consent message is shown, it lists the advertising technology providers involved, and information may also be shared with them according to the choices you make in that message. Apart from what this section describes, the App does not provide your information to anyone.

To learn more about Google's practices:

5. Consent and your advertising choices

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent for advertising. Each time the App starts, it checks with Google whether consent is required in your region. Where it is, Google's consent message is shown before ads are requested, and you can accept or refuse personalized advertising. If you do not consent, Google may show non-personalized or limited ads, depending on your choices and on the rules of your region.

How to change your choices:

  • In the App, open Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy options. This option appears only where the rules of your region require it.
  • In your device's settings, you can reset or delete your Android advertising ID. The exact location of this option depends on your device and Android version.

7. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, such as Argentina's Personal Data Protection Law No. 25,326, the European Union's or the United Kingdom's General Data Protection Regulation, Brazil's General Data Protection Law, or the privacy laws of some U.S. states, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information about you, and have it corrected or deleted;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, certain processing;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what was done before;
  • opt out of the use of your information for targeted advertising, where that right exists; and
  • lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

How to exercise them:

  • Information on your device: you control it directly. You can delete your custom kits in the App, or clear the App's data or uninstall it in your device's settings.
  • Advertising information processed by Google: use the options in section 5 and the tools described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Because we do not keep personal information on any server, we may be unable to identify you or locate information about you; in that case, we will explain how to address your request to Google. We will reply within the time limits required by the law that applies to you.

In Argentina, the authority in charge of the protection of personal data is the Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública. In the European Union and the United Kingdom, you can contact the data protection authority of your country.
